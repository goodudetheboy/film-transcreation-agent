## 08:30 — Launch trailer (video/launch-trailer)

User asked for a launch trailer of at most 3 minutes, aimed at professional
localizers at big-budget studios, with visuals generated as code/SVG rather
than image-model output, living in `video/launch-trailer/`, shipped straight
to `main`.

What changed:
- New standalone Remotion package at
  [video/launch-trailer/](../../video/launch-trailer/README.md). It is not an npm
  workspace, so the app's install and tests are unaffected. It renders a 2:54
  1080p30 MP4 with 15 scenes. Story: cold open, *Inside Out* broccoli →
  green-pepper example, translation vs transcreation, market-pressure stats,
  "every stage has a tool except this one", then the title. Walkthrough:
  Import → Discover → Target → Research → Decide → Converse. Close:
  human-in-the-loop principle, scale across markets, end card.
- All graphics are React + inline SVG: the brand mark, 33 hand-drawn flags, the
  broccoli/pepper line art, and a recreation of the app UI (window chrome,
  details table, player, NLE timeline, review-queue modal, rubric cards,
  research chat). Colors are mirrored from `frontend/src/index.css`, and the
  font is the app's own Satoshi.
- The film excerpts in the player are 7 short silent clips of *Sprite Fright*
  (CC BY 4.0, credited on the end card), cut from `upload/` by
  `scripts/extract-clips.mjs` and committed (~6 MB). The timeline track uses
  the film's real `.srt` timings.
- The score is original and synthesized by `scripts/generate-score.mjs`
  (additive pads, Karplus–Strong plucks, impacts, Schroeder reverb, ffmpeg
  loudnorm to -16 LUFS). Its hits line up with the scene cuts. It is generated
  at render time and git-ignored.
- The UI data came from the live demo project, reached through the
  password-free `?demo=1` link: the Japan items and importance scores, and the
  "Sugar Buns" rubric scores and 落ち着いて、ハニー。 suggestion. Other rows
  (freeform findings, chat exchange, search queries) are illustrative.

Decisions: no ADR. This is marketing collateral outside the app's
architecture. No voice-over, because no TTS was available that would sound
professional, so the trailer uses on-screen typography instead.

## 09:00 — Trailer: cinematic score, realistic broccoli and pepper

User feedback: the music's beat felt jarring, and the broccoli and green
pepper line art looked ugly.

- Score: removed the EDM-style kit (kick on every beat, hi-hats, claps) and the
  plucked arpeggio. The walkthrough now runs on a legato bowed-string
  ostinato over sustained strings, with deep taiko hits only on phrase
  downbeats and a sparse piano motif. The gap scene uses a high violin
  tremolo. The title and final hits each get a low brass "braam". The build
  into the end card is a taiko roll crescendo instead of a drum loop. The
  breakdown was tamed so the "never silently changes anything" beat lands
  quietly. Mix is still -16 LUFS integrated with -1.4 dB peaks.
- Produce: added `src/components/Produce.tsx`, which replaces the line art.
  The broccoli crown is built from domed florets whose buds follow a
  golden-angle phyllotaxis spiral, which is how real broccoli beads pack.
  Each bud is lit from its normal on a hemisphere against an upper-left key
  light, with ambient occlusion and slight organic jitter. The stalk has
  fibre texture and a shadow cast by the canopy. The pepper is a glossy
  lobed body with crease shadows, layered specular streaks, a cool rim
  light, a shoulder cavity, a calyx and a stem with a cut end. Both rest on
  a floor light pool with contact shadows, and both rise in with a soft
  float instead of the line-drawing animation.

Verified via rendered stills and close crops of both objects, plus a full
re-render (2:54, audio checked with volumedetect). The MP4 is still not
committed.

## 09:40 — Trailer: upbeat score with transition beats between features

The user changed direction on the music. They now want it upbeat and hype,
with a clear transition beat between features so viewers know a new one has
started. This supersedes the orchestral score from the 09:00 entry.

- `scripts/generate-score.mjs` is rewritten as a 126 BPM track in D minor
  (Dm–Bb–F–C). It has a four-on-the-floor kick, claps on 2 and 4, offbeat
  open hats, an offbeat bass, supersaw pads that pump with the kick
  (sidechain ducking), a 16th-note arp, and a lead hook in Title, Target,
  Decide, Converse and the finale. Wavetable sines keep generation to about
  6 s.
- Each section restarts its bar grid at its own start time, so every scene
  cut lands on a downbeat. The last 2 beats before a feature cut drop the
  kick and bass and play a snare-roll fill with a reverse cymbal. The cut
  lands on a crash, a kick and a sub impact.
- The story act builds up in layers: a pulse at 10s, half-time at 24s,
  four-on-the-floor at 34s, and a snare-roll build at 46s into the title
  drop at 57s. The breakdown at 148s removes the drums, the build at 156s
  runs into the final drop at 165s, and a last stab rings out.
- The visual transition is `CutFlash` in `LaunchTrailer.tsx`: a skewed light
  sweep plus a brief flash that peaks on each cut frame. It is full strength
  on feature cuts and lighter on story cuts.
- The mix moves to -14 LUFS for hype; the rendered MP4 measures a -13.5 dB
  mean and a -0.5 dB peak.

Verified by zoomed waveforms around the 64s cut (the fill is audible before
the hit), a still on the 75s cut frame, a typecheck and a full render.
